# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)

The first release.

- Word suggestions, next-word prediction and autocorrect with undo, in
  several languages at once: Dutch, English, French, German, Italian,
  Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal) and Spanish; Danish, Finnish, Norwegian
  Bokmål, Romanian and Swedish as experimental. The languages come in two
  packages of their own, `parrotfish-latin-languages` and
  `parrotfish-latin-languages-experimental`.
- Learning on the phone: your words, the words after them, and corrections
  you undo. Learned words can be seen and forgotten, per language; the
  `parrotfish` command shows the words being learned.
- Four layouts in Jolla's list: Auto (QWERTY), Auto (QWERTZ), Auto (AZERTY)
  and Auto (QWERTY ÅÖÄ).
- Settings in Text input: typing languages, autocorrect strength, learning,
  and the look of the keys with a live preview.
